How to play: In a two player round, the questioner sees the word and invents a short German question. The partner answers. Tap the audio buttons for pronunciation and reveal the example only when needed. Give a point for a good answer, or choose “Practice more.” Then pass the screen to the other questioner. You can change themes at any time.
Cards combine your three speaking trainer files with the game’s earlier word set. The sample questions and answers from your trainers are preserved. Audio uses the German speech voice installed in your browser; for best results, enable sound and choose a German voice above. A browser without German speech synthesis may not play audio. Online rooms need internet on both devices. Solo and same screen practice work offline after the file is downloaded, if your browser has a German speech voice installed.
